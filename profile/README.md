# Electrum - Light and Fast Bitcoin Wallet

## About Electrum

Electrum is a lightweight Bitcoin wallet built for people who want speed, control, and a clear view of their coins. Electrum keeps private keys on your own machine, connects to the Bitcoin network through public servers, and lets you send, receive, and review transactions without syncing a full node. Whether you are setting up your first wallet or restoring an old one, Electrum wallet setup stays simple, and Electrum wallet recovery with a seed phrase keeps your funds reachable. Electrum bitcoin wallet sessions are quick to open, quiet on resources, and easy to read for everyday use.

## Quick Start with Electrum

1. **Open Electrum** – Launch Electrum and choose to create a new wallet or restore an existing one.
2. **Set up or restore** – For a new wallet, write down the seed. For Electrum wallet recovery, enter your existing seed.
3. **Secure the seed** – Store the seed phrase offline, never in screenshots or cloud notes.
4. **Receive coins** – Open the Receive tab and share a fresh address.
5. **Send with care** – Review the amount and Electrum transaction fees before confirming.
6. **Rescan if needed** – After restoring, allow Electrum to rebuild history from your seed.
